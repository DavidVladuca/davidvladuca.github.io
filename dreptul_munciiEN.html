Advice and representation in employment law

We advise, assist, and represent both employees and employers, promptly identifying the most practical solution. Employment law defines the rights and obligations of employees and employers in the workplace. The experience of our lawyers, who are members of the Bucharest Bar, supports our approach to each situation through personalised services and attention to detail.

Support for employees and employers

We have experience advising employees on managing and resolving workplace problems, both independently and with our assistance in mediation and alternative dispute resolution. Whatever their position within an organisation, every client receives legal assistance and representation, as well as consideration and support at every stage.
