Advice and representation in corporate and commercial law:

Our law firm’s corporate law specialists advise and assist Romanian and foreign companies established in Romania. We offer solutions concerning:

  • Preparing and drafting the documents required to incorporate a company;
  • Changing or deregistering registered offices and business premises;
  • Obtaining authorisation for business activities;
  • Transfers of shares and ownership interests;
  • Exclusion and withdrawal of company members;
  • Registration and deregistration of branches.

Our services include preparing and drafting articles of association, amendments, and documents for mergers and acquisitions, as well as preparing, drafting, and reviewing commercial contracts.
