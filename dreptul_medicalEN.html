Advice and representation in medical law and medical malpractice

We provide legal assistance and representation in medical malpractice cases, both in disciplinary proceedings and before the malpractice commission, as well as in litigation concerning the tort liability of medical professionals and compensation for financial and non-financial loss.

Our medical malpractice services include:

  • Drafting complaints to the Romanian College of Physicians and the Higher Commission of Physicians;
  • Assistance and representation before the malpractice commission;
  • Challenging the dismissal of a medical malpractice complaint;
  • Challenging the malpractice commission’s decision;
  • Legal assistance and representation before the competent courts in tort claims against medical professionals and providers of medical, healthcare, and pharmaceutical services, including compensation for financial and non-financial loss;
  • Assistance and representation before the competent courts in claims requiring medical professionals to release medical records;
  • Assistance and representation in proceedings concerning the criminal liability of medical professionals;
  • Legal assistance and representation in mediation.

The quality of our law firm’s services is our calling card. We offer solutions tailored to each client’s needs.
