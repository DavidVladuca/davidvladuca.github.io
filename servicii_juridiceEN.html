OUR FEES

Our approach to costs is flexible. Fees reflect the difficulty, scope, urgency, and duration of the matter.

hourly fee: an amount payable to the lawyer for each hour of professional services provided to the client;

fixed fee: a set amount payable to the lawyer for a professional service or category of services provided to the client;

success fee: a fixed or variable amount agreed for achieving a particular outcome or result. A success fee may be agreed in addition to an hourly or fixed fee.

How we set our fees

When setting fees for each client, whether an individual or a business, we take the following factors into account:

  • Whether accepting the client’s instructions prevents the lawyer from accepting instructions from another person, where the client can establish this without further investigation;

  • The time and amount of work required to carry out the client’s instructions or provide the requested services;

  • Time constraints arising from the circumstances of the case that require the lawyer to act promptly to provide quality professional services;

  • The deadlines and the times of day when the professional work must be carried out.

  • Collaboration with experts or other specialists required by the nature, subject matter, complexity, and difficulty of the case;

  • The benefits and results achieved for the client through the lawyer’s work;

  • The client’s social circumstances and financial situation;

  • The importance of the client’s interests in the matter;

  • The type, nature, novelty, and difficulty of the case;
