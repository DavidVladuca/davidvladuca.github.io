Advice and representation in civil law

Our lawyers are members of the Bucharest Bar and have extensive experience in litigation concerning debt recovery, property claims, inheritance, contracts, and personal injury.

Debt recovery

We provide assistance with debt recovery both before litigation and during court proceedings.

Out-of-court debt recovery includes:

  • Drafting and serving payment notices under Article 1015 of the Romanian Code of Civil Procedure;
  • Assistance and representation in negotiating the terms and conditions for recovering outstanding amounts;
  • Drafting settlement agreements and payment undertakings.

During litigation, we provide assistance and representation in recovering debts through:

  • Small claims proceedings;
  • Payment order proceedings;
  • Claims for payment;
  • Claims for the performance of an obligation.

Once a final judgment or an enforceable instrument has been obtained, our law firm can represent creditors or debtors in enforcement proceedings before a bailiff. Our work includes filing enforcement applications, proposing payment schedules, monitoring the collection of sums due, and representation in challenges to enforcement.

Civil contracts

Under the Romanian Civil Code, a contract is an agreement between two or more persons intended to create, amend, or extinguish a legal relationship. Understanding the applicable legislation is important, and legal assistance can be useful.

Our team can assist you with civil contracts and contracts between professionals, including:

  • Agreements for the sale and purchase of movable and immovable property;
  • Lease agreements;
  • Loan-for-use agreements.

We negotiate terms and conditions, review contracts, and assist with claims concerning termination, rescission, or annulment. We also provide representation in proceedings involving:

  • Property boundaries and recovery of real property;
  • Claims for payment and performance of obligations;
  • Division of property and inheritance;
  • Challenges to enforcement, including enforcement of tax debts;
  • Divorce.

Our law firm can propose solutions to your legal problems, drawing on our professionalism, experience, and the quality of the services we have provided over the years.
