We offer the following legal services:

  • Legal advice on administrative offences;
  • Legal advice, assistance, and representation in seeking the annulment of administrative offence reports relating to road traffic, employment, environmental law, and other offences governed by special legislation;
  • Drafting and presenting challenges to administrative offence reports;
  • Legal assistance and representation in court proceedings challenging administrative offence reports on their merits, as well as in appeals;
  • Legal assistance and representation in proceedings seeking annulment of administrative offence reports on grounds of limitation of liability or lapse of the report;
  • Obtaining court registry certificates for the recovery of driving licences retained by enforcement officers following an administrative penalty;
  • Other services concerning administrative offences;
